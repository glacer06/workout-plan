ORIGINAL B ICON SET (blue)

The final mark is candidate B: dark navy faceted kettlebell with a bold white S.
(The app was renamed from Steph Fitness to Sims Fitness on Sept 26, 2026. File names below were kept so nothing breaks.)

Files:
  steph-fitness-B-blue.png          Exact approved 1024 x 1024 electric-blue raster master, unchanged.
  steph-fitness-B-white.png         1024 x 1024 PNG with a white background and soft ground shadow.
  steph-fitness-B-transparent.png   1024 x 1024 PNG with real alpha (transparent background and handle opening).
  steph-fitness-B-blue.svg          Hand-built, editable vector adaptation on electric blue.
  steph-fitness-B-white.svg         Same vector mark on white with ground shadow.
  steph-fitness-B-transparent.svg   Same vector mark with real transparency and no ground shadow.
  favicon.svg                       Tighter crop of the blue mark for browser tabs.

The SVGs are deliberate flat vector redraws of B's silhouette, facets and white S, not pixel-for-pixel 3D copies of the raster renders. The white and transparent PNGs are cut-out adaptations of B; their contours may look slightly softer than the original blue master. For the exact approved image, use steph-fitness-B-blue.png.
